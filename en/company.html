Message from the CEOs

We put our customers' sites first

Thank you for visiting JUNIN INDUSTRY Co., Ltd..

Founded in 2016, JUNIN is a manufacturer dedicated to steel grating. At our head office and plant in Hwaseong, Gyeonggi-do, we manage the entire process, from receiving raw material through automatic projection welding, fabrication, hot-dip galvanizing and inspection to shipment. Over the past ten years we have delivered 37,300 tons of grating and stair treads to more than 350 customers.

Our grating is in service where safety matters most: petrochemical plants, power stations, data centers and airports. Every product is made to the customer's design, with tolerances per NAAMM and quality and environmental management certified to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.

We will keep our promise of "skill and trust" and continue to put our customers' sites first.

Lee Deok-geun and Koo Hyung-geun, Co-CEOs, JUNIN INDUSTRY Co., Ltd.
