Annual Delivery

Over 3,000 tons delivered every year since 2018

Product Mix

What we deliver

Over the same period, 95% of volume (approx. 35,400 t) was grating and 5% (approx. 1,950 t) was stair treads. By bearing bar type, I-bar and serrated I-bar account for 70%.
