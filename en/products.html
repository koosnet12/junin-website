Structure

How steel grating is built

Steel grating is an open-grid floor panel. Load-carrying bearing bars stand on edge at a fixed spacing (pitch), and cross bars run across them, joined into a single piece by electric resistance projection welding.

It lets light, air and rainwater through and has a high strength-to-weight ratio, which makes it the standard choice for plant walkways, platforms, trench covers and stairs. The panel ends are either closed with end bars (banded type) or left open (open type).

Types

Bearing bar profiles

Grating is classified into four types by bearing bar profile. Each is available as banded type or open type.

I-BAR

I-Bar Grating

Grating whose bearing bars have an I-shaped cross section.

FLAT BAR

Flat Bar Grating

Grating whose bearing bars are plain rectangular flat bars.

SERRATED I-BAR

Serrated I-Bar Grating

Grating whose bearing bars combine an I-shaped section with a serrated top edge.

SERRATED

Serrated Grating

Grating whose flat bearing bars have a serrated top edge.

Hot-dip Galvanizing

Hot-dip galvanizing

After fabrication, the grating is dipped in molten zinc so that every surface is covered with a zinc coating. This protects it from corrosion outdoors and in humid plant environments.

Galvanizing is done to KS D 8308, JIS H8641 and ASTM A123, and coating thickness and surface condition are measured before shipment.
