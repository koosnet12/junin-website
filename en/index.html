About

From rolled bar to shipment,
one site, one continuous flow

Founded in 2016, JUNIN has focused on one product: steel grating. At our plant in Hwaseong, Gyeonggi-do, we control every step in house: automatic press welding, fabrication, hot-dip galvanizing, three inspections and packing.

Every panel is made to the customer's drawings, with dimensional tolerances per NAAMM (National Association of Architectural Metal Manufacturers). Our quality and environmental management systems are certified to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.

Process

A production line built on three automatic press welders

Three 2,230 kVA automatic press-welding machines run in parallel, so we can handle large project volumes and tight delivery dates at the same time.

Facility & capacity
  1. STEP 1

    Press Welding

    Bearing bars and cross bars are joined into one piece by electric resistance press welding.

  2. STEP 2

    High-speed Cutting

    Panels are cut to the dimensions on the drawing.

  3. STEP 3

    Shape Cutting

    Cut-outs are made for pipes and columns.

  4. STEP 4

    Welding

    End bars and cut-out banding are welded on.

  5. STEP 5

    Grinding & Inspection

    Welds are ground and checked against NAAMM tolerances.

  6. STEP 6

    Galvanizing & Shipment

    Hot-dip galvanized, inspected and shipped.
