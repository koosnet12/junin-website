10 Steps

Ten-step production process

Every product is made to the customer's drawings and inspected three times before shipment. Red numbers mark the inspection steps.

  1. 01

    Raw Material

    SS400 and SS275 rolled bars are sourced from Korean rolling mills.

  2. 02

    Press Welding

    Bearing bars and cross bars are joined by electric resistance press welding.

  3. 03

    Fabrication

    Panels are cut to drawing dimensions; end bars and cut-out banding are welded.

  4. 04

    Fabrication Inspection

    Dimensions and welds are checked against NAAMM tolerances.

  5. 05

    Galvanizing

    Hot-dip galvanized to KS D 8308, JIS H8641 and ASTM A123.

  6. 06

    Coating Inspection

    Coating thickness and surface condition are measured.

  7. 07

    Final Inspection

    Quantity, fabrication marks and final dimensions are verified.

  8. 08

    Packing

    Bundled and marked in installation sequence.

  9. 09

    Delivery

    Shipped to site on the scheduled delivery date.

  10. 10

    Installation

    Installed on site with fixing clips and similar fasteners.
